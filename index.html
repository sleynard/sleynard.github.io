About the writer

Stephen Leynard

Stephen is a recent graduate of Vancouver Island University in Canada, where he earned a BA (Honours) in Psychology and an Addictions Certificate.

Through Mind Over Matter, he shares psychological topics in clear, engaging language for readers interested in how people think, feel, and behave.

He is interested in pursuing a PhD in Clinical Psychology and sees psychology as a long-term professional focus.

Educational information

A note for readers

Content on Mind Over Matter is provided for educational and informational purposes only. These essays reflect the author’s personal interpretations and opinions, and readers are encouraged to consider other perspectives, evaluate sources independently, and form their own informed conclusions. The content is not medical or mental-health advice and should not replace care from a qualified health or mental-health professional for personal concerns.

Editorial standards

Artificial-intelligence tools assisted with this website’s design and development. They are not used to draft or write the essays, which are authored by Stephen Leynard and reflect his original thought, analysis, and voice. Where appropriate and practicable, supporting sources are cited in APA style.

Reader privacy

Privacy

A short explanation of what is stored when you create a Mind Over Matter reader account.

Account information

Your email address is used for secure sign-in and is not shown publicly. Your chosen display name, comments, and likes may appear publicly beside the articles you interact with.

Where it is stored

Account, community, and email-preference information is stored in Supabase. Your email subscription is private and is not displayed to other readers. Publication emails are delivered through Resend. Your browser also remembers your theme and sign-in session on your device.

Publication emails

Email updates are optional. You will receive them only if you actively select the email option while creating an account or later enable it in your reader account. Every publication email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also choose whether to receive essays, book recommendations, both, or neither from your account.

Readership measurement

Mind Over Matter uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand general readership, such as page views and referring sites. It is configured as privacy-focused site measurement and does not create advertising profiles on this website.

Your choices

You can remove your own likes and comments and change or withdraw your email consent at any time. To ask for your reader account to be deleted, contact hello@stephenleynard.com.
