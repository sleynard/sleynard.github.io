About

Mind Over Matter

An independent collection of essays by Stephen Leynard about psychology, behavior, and the quiet mechanics of thought.

The goal is simple: explore how people think, feel, choose, and change in language that is thoughtful, useful, and easy to follow.

These essays are educational and reflective. They are not medical advice, diagnosis, or a substitute for support from a qualified professional.
